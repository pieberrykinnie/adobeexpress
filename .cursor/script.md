# BrandGenie Hackathon Demo Script

## 1. Introduction (0:00–0:20)
- Hi, I’m [Your Name], and this is **BrandGenie**—an AI-powered Adobe Express Add-on that instantly generates a complete Brand Kit from your logo or website.
- Our goal: eliminate the friction of brand setup for small businesses, educators, and creators.

## 2. Problem Statement (0:20–0:40)
- Creating a consistent brand look is hard—manual, time-consuming, and requires design expertise.
- Many users skip Brand Kits, leading to off-brand content and lost trust.

## 3. Solution Overview (0:40–1:10)
- BrandGenie lets you upload a logo or enter a website URL.
- In seconds, it analyzes your brand’s colors, fonts, and imagery style using AI and Adobe Firefly.
- It previews a palette and font set, then creates a ready-to-use Brand Kit in Express—no design skills needed.

## 4. Live Demo (1:10–2:20)
- [Show the Add-on panel in Adobe Express.]
- Upload a logo or enter a website URL.
- Click “Analyze”—see the extracted palette and font suggestions.
- Click “Create Brand Kit”—the kit is created and available in Express.
- Show the BrandGuard linter: try to use an off-brand color or font, and see the real-time warning.

## 5. Technical Highlights (2:20–2:40)
- Uses a Cloudflare Worker for fast logo/URL analysis and palette extraction.
- Integrates with Adobe Firefly for AI-enhanced palettes and template generation.
- BrandGuard linter ensures on-brand design in real time.

## 6. Impact & Launch Plan (2:40–3:00)
- BrandGenie saves hours for every new brand, making professional branding accessible to all.
- Ready for production: just connect your Adobe and Firefly API keys.
- We’re excited to launch and help millions of Express users create on-brand content, fast!
