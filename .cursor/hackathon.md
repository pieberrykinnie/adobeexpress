# Adobe Express Add-ons Hackathon

## Overview / Details
The **Adobe Express Add-ons Hackathon** invites developers, designers, and creative builders to shape the future of the Adobe Express platform by proposing innovative "Add-ons"—mini-apps that expand Express’ features, workflows, or integrations. Participants will design and pitch an idea, prove its technical feasibility within the Express extensibility framework, and demonstrate readiness to build and ship if selected.

Express is a quick-and-easy design application used by business professionals, marketers, educators, entrepreneurs, and more to create standout content in minutes. The hackathon emphasizes creativity, user value, and demonstrable intent to deliver.

## Problem Statement
Create a compelling Adobe Express Add-on that:
1. Provides significant value to Adobe Express users (either broad or niche).
2. Demonstrates creativity, innovation, or a unique workflow.
3. Is technically feasible on the Adobe Express platform (prototype or proof-of-concept required).
4. Can be shipped to production if awarded a prize.

## Required Deliverables
1. **Pitch Video** (≈3 minutes; judges will only watch the first 3 minutes)
   • Introduce yourself and the Add-on idea, target audience, and problem solved.
   • Demonstrate core features running in an Express Add-on environment (local project or Code Playground).
   • Outline a concise launch plan if you win.

2. **Optional Supporting Materials** (strengthen submission but not mandatory)
   • Mockups, sketches, or images.
   • Private link to proof-of-concept Add-on or code.
   • Build plans, technical docs, user research/insights, etc.

## Judging Criteria
1. **Value for Express Users** – Target user fit and practical benefit.
2. **Innovation / Uniqueness** – Creativity in tech, design, or workflow; differentiation from existing solutions.
3. **Feasibility & Intent to Build** – Prototype evidence and clear launch plan.
4. **Relevance to Prize Categories** – Alignment with selected prize theme(s).
5. **Clarity of Add-on Concept** – Ease of understanding for new Express users; clear communication in video.

## Prize Categories & Awards
Each category awards **$5,000 USD** plus mentorship and a feature on the Adobe Developers Blog (1 winner per category):
1. Most Valuable to Express Users
2. Best AI-Enhanced Experience
3. Best Integration or Workflow Hack
4. Best New Video Innovation
5. Collaboration Catalyst
6. "Express Yourself" Wildcard

_Total prize pool: **$30,000 USD**_
