## Iteration 1

**Search queries used**
- "Adobe Express add-on ideas 2025"
- "Adobe Express extensibility SDK features 2025"
- "Winning Adobe hackathon Express add-ons 2024"
- "Best AI enhanced design tools 2025 trends"
- "Content scheduler automation Express add-on idea"

**Key insights gathered**
- Adobe is heavily promoting Generative AI capabilities in Express (image, text effects, object removal, expansion, etc.) through Firefly.
- The Content Scheduler is a differentiating feature but lacks advanced AI-driven optimization (best posting times, A/B testing captions, automatic generation of social copy).
- Bulk Create & Generate add-on allows spreadsheet-driven templating; however, integrations for dynamic data sources (e.g., e-commerce feeds, CMS, CRMs) remain scarce.
- Collaboration features (comments, approvals) exist, yet real-time co-editing and guided design feedback are limited.
- Recent Adobe/Microsoft partnership shows appetite for “agents” that embed Express in other ecosystems; an add-on that brings third-party data or AI insights into Express could align with this vision.

**Early idea seeds**
1. Brand Style Guide Wizard – generates on-brand color palettes, typography pairings, and reusable template packs based on a company logo or URL.
2. AI Social Caption Copilot – suggests engaging, on-brand social captions, hashtags, and alt-text for any design, optimized per platform & posting time.
3. Translate & Localize – one-click bulk translation of text layers across pages using modern LLMs, preserving style and layout.
4. Smart A/B Post Optimizer – creates multiple copy/design variants, schedules them, and reports on performance to iteratively improve content.
5. Data-Driven Template Filler – connects Google Sheets / Airtable / Shopify to dynamically populate templates for product catalogs, ads, or posts.
